## Common Interior Car Stains You Shouldn’t Ignore

Different stains require different treatment approaches. Acting quickly helps prevent permanent damage to upholstery, carpets, and interior surfaces.


**Coffee and Soft Drinks**
Dark, sugary liquids absorb quickly and often leave sticky residue and lingering odors if not addressed promptly.


**Mud and Dirt**
Frequently tracked in by shoes, kids, or pets, dirt embeds deep into carpet fibers and can harden if left untreated.


**Food Grease and Oils**
Fast food spills and oily residues attract dirt and discolor fabric over time.


**Ink Stains**
Leaking pens can permanently mark seats and door panels if mishandled.


**Pet Accidents and Odors**
Pet hair, stains, and odors are difficult to remove without specialized tools and cleaners.

## DIY Cleanup: What You Can Handle At Home

Light stains and fresh spills can often be minimized with proper at-home care. These steps are ideal for surface-level maintenance, not deep restoration.

### Coffee and Beverage Spills

Blot immediately using a clean, dry cloth never scrub. Apply a mild dish soap and warm water solution using gentle dabbing motions, then rinse lightly and air-dry.

### Mud and Dirt

Allow mud to dry completely before brushing it loose. Vacuum thoroughly, then wipe remaining residue with a damp cloth and mild cleaner.

### Grease and Oil

Sprinkle baking soda or cornstarch to absorb oils. After vacuuming, lightly clean with a grease-cutting upholstery-safe cleaner.

### Ink Marks

Use rubbing alcohol sparingly on a cotton pad. Press gently and lift do not rub to prevent spreading the stain.



**Important Note:** DIY methods are best for minor stains only. Over-wetting, harsh chemicals, or aggressive scrubbing can permanently damage interior materials.
